A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Before we begin.

Do you offer emergency service?+

Yes. Call 214-774-7604 for 24-hour emergency service throughout the DFW Metroplex. Tell us the property address, type of damage and whether the situation is ongoing. Arrival timing depends on location and current availability.

What happens after I contact MC?+

We discuss the situation and arrange the next appropriate step. The work scope, site conditions and material needs determine the project plan and scheduling.

Can you handle cleanup and reconstruction?+

Yes. MC offers water mitigation, fire and smoke cleanup, and reconstruction. The services needed for your property are established during assessment and scope planning.

What should I send with an inquiry?+

Include your address or city, a description of the issue, your contact details and any photos you can safely take. Existing plans or insurance repair documents can also help explain the requested scope.

Do you work on homes and commercial properties?+

Yes. Services cover residential and commercial restoration, repairs and improvements.

Can a project include several services?+

Yes. For example, water damage can require drying, material removal and reconstruction. Note all affected areas in your project brief so the scope can be considered together.

What should I have ready for a project discussion?+

The property location, photos of the affected areas, a short description of the work and any relevant plans or insurance repair documents are helpful starting points.
